Parkour was a much bigger part of my life when I was younger. I ran an
Instagram page where I posted a new trick every day for over 1000 days
straight. What draws me to it is the freedom: it's something I can do almost
anywhere. My favorite trick is the side flip, while the double backflip is my
least favorite, since I tend to lose my orientation mid-rotation and have
been injured attempting it more than once.
